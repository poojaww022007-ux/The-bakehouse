Our Story

A little bakehouse with big-hearted baking

The Bakehouse is a premium bakery and cake shop in Akola, Maharashtra, known for its indulgent cheesecake pastries, rich brownies, celebration cakes and freshly baked treats. Every item is made in small batches using quality ingredients — real cream cheese, Belgian chocolate, fresh fruit and slow-baked bases — so each bite tastes as beautiful as it looks.

Eggless & Veg FriendlyMost of our range is pure vegetarian and eggless.
